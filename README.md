→ On-time delivery was 85.8% against a 95.0% target, with 142 shipments late.
→ Penalties and claims totaled $27,952, or 0.8% of revenue.
→ 21 lanes were below the margin floor, and 65 shipments were flagged as priority risk.
→ 50 shipments were flagged as unusual for audit, while own-fleet empty return km equaled 22.7% of loaded km.

The data indicates a structural margin problem: delay risk, empty-return miles, and weak lane economics are compounding before revenue is converted into profit. The operating pattern is consistent across multiple shippers and lanes, which points to process controls and pricing discipline rather than isolated exceptions.
